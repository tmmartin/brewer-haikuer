About

Like many, I owe my Milwaukee Brewer fandom to Bob Uecker. In 2005 we bought our family home, and I spent many weeks prior to moving in cleaning and getting ready. This meant fresh coats of paint in all the rooms. Never much of a baseball fan, I found myself listening to the games as I rolled the walls and gradually followed the play by play. I literally became interested in the Brewers while watching paint dry. But the stories are what hooked me. It didn’t matter who the Brewers played or what the score was - you didn’t want to miss a Uecker broadcast. In fact the more lopsided the score, the better - more room for his wandering memories and stories of the game.

I first thought to pen a haiku about a Brewer game in 2009, not long after getting an account on Twitter, and thinking to myself, “the only thing this short-form online message thing could possibly be good for is sports-based Japanese poetry”.

Thus began a journey of many years chronicling my beloved local team. In 2011, with the Brewers headed to the NLCS, the local media was publishing any and every Brewer related story. Mine is in the Journal Sentinel archive:

I quit the haiku after the 2024 season, but obviously the Crew kept on winning and are headed to the 2026 World Series as I publish this site. Look for future updates to let anyone submit a game haiku!
